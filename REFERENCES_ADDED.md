## Looked for but not added

- **Dua & Graff, "UCI Machine Learning Repository" (2019).** The current UCI
  site no longer prescribes this generic form and instead gives a per-dataset
  DOI citation, so the generic entry was dropped; Adult and Crime are cited
  through their originating papers (`kohavi1996scaling`, `redmond2002data`)
  instead.
- **Caton & Haas, "Fairness in Machine Learning: A Survey" (ACM CSUR).** The
  ACM Digital Library record returned HTTP 403 to every fetch, so the volume,
  issue and article number could not be confirmed from a publisher page; left out.
- **Page numbers for `louppe2017pivot`, `moyer2018invariant`, `klambauer2017selu`
  and `ding2021retiring` (NeurIPS years without fixed pagination on the official
  site) and for `kipf2017semi` (ICLR).** The proceedings pages carry no
  `firstpage`/`lastpage` metadata for these, so the entries omit `pages` rather
  than assert an unverified range.
- **A DOI for `silverman1986density`.** Only the later Routledge/CRC reissue has
  one (10.1201/9781315140919); the 1986 edition predates DOI assignment, so the
  entry carries no DOI.
- **A DOI for `kohavi1996scaling`.** ACM DL shows a 10.5555 identifier, which is
  the reserved non-resolving prefix rather than a registered DOI; the entry
  points at the AAAI KDD-96 PDF instead.
